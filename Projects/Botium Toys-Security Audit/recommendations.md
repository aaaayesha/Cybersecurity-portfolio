# Security Recommendations

Based on the results of the internal IT security audit and controls assessment, the following recommendations are prioritized to address high-risk gaps and improve Botium Toys’ overall security posture.

## Priority Recommendations

- Enforce least privilege and separation of duties to limit unauthorized access to sensitive systems and data
- Implement encryption for cardholder data and PII/SPII to protect confidentiality and support regulatory compliance
- Establish disaster recovery and backup procedures to ensure business continuity in the event of system failure or security incidents
- Deploy an intrusion detection system (IDS) to improve visibility into potential threats and anomalous activity
- Strengthen password policies and implement a centralized password management system to reduce credential-related risk
- Formalize legacy system monitoring and maintenance schedules to mitigate vulnerabilities in end-of-life systems
- Classify assets to better align security controls with business risk and regulatory requirements

## Expected Impact

Implementing these recommendations would significantly reduce the organization’s risk exposure, strengthen alignment with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC requirements, and lower the likelihood of security incidents, operational disruption, and regulatory penalties.
