# Audit Scope and Goals

## Scope
The scope of this internal IT security audit covers the entire security program at Botium Toys. This includes employee devices, on-premises equipment, internal networks, business systems, and data storage environments managed by the IT department.

## Goals
The goals of this audit were to:
- Assess IT-managed assets
- Evaluate existing security controls
- Identify compliance gaps
- Complete a controls and compliance checklist
- Recommend improvements to strengthen the organization’s security posture
